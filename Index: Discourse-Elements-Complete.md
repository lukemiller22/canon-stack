# Complete Discourse Elements Framework
## 11 Categories | 152 Elements
**For Agentic Retrieval and Syntopical Analysis**

---

## Category 1: Semantic Elements (14)

Used to identify **meaning-making operations** in text.

1. **Concept** - An abstract or general idea representing fundamental principles
2. **Term** - A word or phrase used unambiguously (Note: Also used in separate Syntopicon layer)
3. **Category** - A class or division grouping items by shared characteristics
4. **Domain** - A distinct sphere of activity, knowledge, or experience
5. **Subject** - A branch of knowledge to be studied or taught
6. **Topic** - A discrete area of focus within a broader subject (Note: Also used in separate Syntopicon layer)
7. **Question** - An interrogative sentence used to review, challenge, or extend knowledge
8. **Example** - A specific instance illustrating a general idea
9. **Context** - Surrounding discourse that illuminates meaning
10. **Quality** - Characteristic features or degree of excellence
11. **Quantity** - Amount or number of something
12. **Definition** - Precise statement of meaning
13. **Description** - Detailed account of characteristics or features
14. **Relationship** - Connection between words, statements, sources, or ideas

---

## Category 2: Logical Elements (12)

Used to identify **reasoning structures and argumentative moves**.

1. **Assumption** - Basic or foundational belief underlying other beliefs
2. **Axiom** - Self-evident proposition that cannot reasonably be denied
3. **Bias** - Predisposition influencing perspective or judgment
4. **Claim** - Assertion expressing a judgment or opinion
5. **Error** - Mistaken belief, judgment, or action from faulty reasoning
6. **Conditional** - Stipulations, conditions, or limitations on claims
7. **Evidence** - Information indicating whether a belief is true or valid
8. **Warrant** - Logical connection between evidence and claim
9. **Qualifier** - Statement limiting the strength or applicability of a claim
10. **Objection** - Reason or argument opposing a belief or proposition
11. **Fallacy** - Error in reasoning based on invalid arguments
12. **Paradox** - Seemingly contradictory statement revealing deeper truth

---

## Category 3: Narrative Elements (16)

Used to identify **story structure and historical/cultural context**.

1. **Origin** - Point or source from which something begins
2. **Time** - Point or period when action or event occurs
3. **Place** - Particular position or location in space
4. **Situation** - Unique circumstances in which event/action takes place
5. **Person** - Individual or character involved in events or discourse
6. **Group** - Collection of individuals with common identity or purpose
7. **Custom** - Traditional way of behaving within a culture
8. **Culture** - Customs, beliefs, arts, and achievements of a people
9. **Event** - Happening or occurrence, especially noteworthy ones
10. **Conflict** - Struggle between opposing forces, ideas, or interests
11. **Action** - Thing done; an act or deed
12. **Quotation** - Exact words spoken or written by a person
13. **Ritual** - Prescribed sequence of actions with symbolic meaning
14. **Artifact** - Object made by humans with cultural/historical interest
15. **Ideology** - Set of beliefs shaping worldview
16. **Telos** - Ultimate end or purpose

---

## Category 4: Personal Elements (26)

Used to identify **first-person expressions and subjective states**.

1. **Identity** (I am) - Sense of self encompassing history and characteristics
2. **Thought** (I think, I wonder) - Intellectual processes of contemplation
3. **Emotion** (I love, I hate, I fear) - Complex psychological states driving behavior
4. **Perception** (I see) - Cognitive process interpreting sensory information
5. **Desire** (I want, I seek) - Principles or standards held in high regard
6. **Intention** (I intend, I plan) - Conscious mental state of deliberate purpose
7. **Need** (I need) - Fundamental requirement essential for well-being
8. **Belief** (I believe, I expect) - Acceptance that something is true
9. **Knowledge** (I know, I understand) - Awareness gained by experience or education
10. **Act** (I [verb]) - Execution or performance of a task or deed
11. **Speech** (I say, I reply) - Expression of thoughts through spoken/written words
12. **Ability** (I can/cannot) - Recognition of capacity or skill
13. **Possession** (I have, I own) - State of having or controlling something
14. **Obligation** (I must) - Sense of duty or moral imperative
15. **Gratitude** (I thank, I rejoice) - Expression of appreciation or thankfulness
16. **Worship** (I praise, I adore) - Expression of reverence or devotion
17. **Taste** (I like, I prefer) - Ability to discern quality or aesthetic standard
18. **Imagination** (I imagine) - Cognitive ability to generate novel ideas
19. **Decision** (I choose) - Conclusion reached after consideration
20. **Confession** (I regret, I repent) - Acknowledgment of faults or wrongdoings
21. **Request** (I ask, I plead) - Act of asking earnestly or formally
22. **Lament** (I grieve, I mourn) - Expression of deep sorrow or grief
23. **Experience** (I [past verb]) - Direct observation or participation in events
24. **Memory** (I remember, I recall) - Faculty storing and remembering information
25. **Doubt** (I doubt) - Feeling of uncertainty or lack of conviction
26. **Promise** (I will, I promise) - Declaration or commitment to do something

---

## Category 5: Practical Elements (21)

Used to identify **action-oriented and application-focused content**.

1. **Objective** - Specific and measurable goal or aim
2. **Strategy** - Plan or approach to achieve a goal
3. **Problem** - Issue presenting difficulty requiring resolution
4. **Mental Model** - Simplified representation of how something works
5. **Principle** - Fundamental truth or rule guiding action
6. **Solution** - Means of resolving a problem or difficulty
7. **Constraint** - Limitation or restriction affecting ability to act
8. **Hypothesis** - Proposed explanation testable through investigation
9. **Experiment** - Methodical trial and error process
10. **Task** - Piece of work requiring specific action
11. **Project** - Collaborative planned undertaking
12. **Habit** - Solution-response becoming almost automatic
13. **Exercise** - Structured activity developing skills
14. **Metric** - Standard of measurement quantifying performance
15. **Imperative** - Command or urgent instruction directing action
16. **Tool** - Device or implement carrying out a function
17. **Medium** - Set of presentation elements creating an object/experience
18. **Resource** - Source of help or aid accomplishing a task
19. **Process** - Set of steps followed to accomplish a goal
20. **Theory** - Potential explanation answering Why or How questions
21. **System** - Complex whole with interconnecting parts

---

## Category 6: Symbolic Elements (8)

Used to identify **figurative language and symbolic representation**.

1. **Archetype** - Universally recognized symbol recurring across cultures
2. **Theme** - Underlying message or central idea recurring throughout work
3. **Story** - Narrative account conveying meaning through symbolism
4. **Image** - Visual representation (photograph, drawing, diagram)
5. **Illustration** - Visual explanation of text/concept (diagram, infographic)
6. **Metaphor** - Figure of speech suggesting resemblance or analogy
7. **Idiom** - Phrase with figurative meaning different from literal
8. **Symbol** - Object/character representing an abstract idea

---

## Category 7: Reference Elements (10)

Used to identify **citations, cross-references, and intertextual connections**.

1. **Source** - Origin or provider of information
2. **Reference** - Pointer to specific place within a source
3. **Cross-Reference** - Navigation to another section or related document
4. **Citation** - Direct reproduction of text from a source
5. **Annotation** - Note of explanation or comment added to text
6. **Allusion** - Indirect reference to person, place, thing, or idea
7. **Commentary** - Remarks providing additional understanding
8. **Summary** - Concise representation of main ideas
9. **Paraphrase** - Restatement in different words
10. **Synthesis** - Combination of separate ideas forming coherent whole

---

## Category 8: Structural Elements (6)

Used to identify **organizational and visual structures**.

1. **List** - Sequence of items in specific order
2. **Diagram** - Simplified drawing explaining parts or operation
3. **Map** - Visual depiction of geographical areas or spatial relationships
4. **Table** - Structured arrangement of data in rows and columns
5. **Chart** - Visual representation of numerical data
6. **Timeline** - Graphical representation of chronological sequence

---

## Category 9: Computational Elements (15)

**NEW** - Used to identify **cognitive operations and reasoning patterns** that chunks enable agents to perform.

1. **Algorithm** - Step-by-step procedure accomplishing a specific task
2. **Transformation** - Mapping or conversion changing one form into another
3. **Validation** - Criteria or methods verifying correctness or truth
4. **Aggregation** - Methods combining or integrating multiple pieces of information
5. **Inference** - Logical derivations or conclusions from presented information
6. **Classification** - Categorization schemes or taxonomic frameworks
7. **Extraction** - Information that can be pulled out and used independently
8. **Generation** - Templates or patterns enabling creation of new content
9. **Comparison** - Explicit contrasts, similarities, or evaluative frameworks
10. **Decomposition** - Breaking down complex concepts into constituent parts
11. **Composition** - Building up complex ideas from simpler building blocks
12. **Optimization** - Methods for improving or finding best solutions
13. **Prediction** - Frameworks for forecasting or projecting future states
14. **Diagnosis** - Methods identifying problems, causes, or root issues
15. **Evaluation** - Frameworks for assessing, judging, or measuring value

---

## Category 10: Rhetorical Elements (12)

**NEW** - Used to identify **argumentative structure and persuasive strategies**.

1. **Thesis** - Central claim or position being advanced in an argument
2. **Antithesis** - Contrasting or opposing viewpoint for dialectical purposes
3. **Dialectic** - Synthesis emerging from tension between thesis and antithesis
4. **Stipulation** - Definitions or assumptions stated as ground rules
5. **Concession** - Acknowledgment of valid points in opposing arguments
6. **Amplification** - Expansion or elaboration of a point for emphasis
7. **Qualification** - Nuancing or limiting claims for precision
8. **Appeal-Logos** - Logical reasoning and rational argumentation
9. **Appeal-Ethos** - Credibility or authority-based persuasion
10. **Appeal-Pathos** - Emotional engagement and affective persuasion
11. **Refutation** - Direct rebuttal or dismantling of opposing arguments
12. **Commonplace** - Appeal to shared beliefs, values, or cultural assumptions

---

## Category 11: Query Intent Elements (12)

**NEW** - Used to tag **what kinds of questions chunks answer** (aids intent-based retrieval).

1. **Definitional** - Answers "What is X?" questions
2. **Procedural** - Answers "How do I do X?" questions
3. **Causal** - Answers "Why does X happen?" questions
4. **Comparative** - Answers "How does X compare to Y?" questions
5. **Evaluative** - Answers "Is X good/true/valuable?" questions
6. **Historical** - Answers "What happened?" or "When?" questions
7. **Predictive** - Answers "What will happen?" questions
8. **Diagnostic** - Answers "What's wrong?" questions
9. **Prescriptive** - Answers "What should be done?" questions
10. **Interpretive** - Answers "What does X mean?" questions
11. **Exemplary** - Answers "Can you give me an example?" questions
12. **Synthetic** - Answers "How does it all fit together?" questions

**Application Notes:**

1. **Separate Metadata Layers:**
   - **Concepts** - Fixed list from Index: Concepts.md
   - **Topics** - Flexible Concept/Topic pairs from Index: Topics.md
   - **Terms** - Flexible vocabulary from Index: Terms.md
   - **Scripture References** - Normalized biblical citations
   - **Named Entities** - Person/Place/Event/Group/Work/Period/Ideology
   - **Structure Path** - Document hierarchy breadcrumb

2. **Discourse Elements Layer:**
   - All 152 elements above applied as [[Category/Element]]
   - Multiple elements per chunk typical (3-8)
   - Tagged with brief description or quote

3. **Usage Guidelines:**
   - Elements should be **descriptive, not prescriptive**
   - Tag what **is present**, not what's missing
   - Use namespace format: [[Category/Element]]
   - Add brief content description for context
